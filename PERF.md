## Why the generator repairs instead of retrying

Share of random region layouts (balanced growth around a valid solution) that have exactly one solution, out of 500 per size:

| Size | Unique layouts |
| --- | --- |
| 6x6 | 0.0% |
| 7x7 | 0.0% |
| 8x8 | 0.0% |

A plain retry loop would need hundreds to millions of layouts per puzzle. Instead the generator moves single boundary cells between regions and keeps any move that leaves no more cells unresolved by the deduction engine. When nothing is unresolved, the puzzle is both unique and solvable by logic alone.

## Difficulty mapping

Puzzles solvable with level 1 alone (singles) do not exist without single-cell regions, so: easy = levels 1-2, medium = level 3 with at most 2 level-3 steps, hard = more level-3 steps than that.
